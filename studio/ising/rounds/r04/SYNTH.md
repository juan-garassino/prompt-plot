The one change is **draw the hot half as heat: the same closed hull, shrinking.** This is the Schotter move the HANDOFF's own lineage promises: one element progressively displaced, not an element that stops.

Only if a 10-site hull fails (c) should you fall back to the art critic's short ≥ 2 mm touches for the smallest rung, and say so in NOTES.
2. **Make the weight read.** Widen the 2- and 3-pass offsets until a 3-pass hull inks a solid 0.6–0.8 mm band, at least 2.5× a 1-pass stroke. ## Do not
- **Do not** use dots, specks or singletons for the smallest rung. The r03-era rule against "some other small mark" is revised: a smaller closed hull of the same kind is the same mark, not a new one. Touches only as the declared fallback in instruction 1.
- **Do not** vary the cut with T, fade by random deletion or thin by hand. The gradient must come from the physics at one fixed cut, printed on the sheet.
- **Do not** narrow the whole ladder to satisfy the clearance. Thin only the offending inner edge.
- **Do not** cut a rectangular slot for any text again. Halos follow the glyphs.
- **Do not** print a number the ink cannot show (r04's 0.977 described a blanked band). Measure the printed check on the rendered rows.
- **Do not** bring back bonds, a chart, a deck or more ruler labels. Do not let the new rung delete or recolour any coast edge.
