## Reads at a glance

At 3 m a stranger sees a big blue-and-gold string hyperboloid bursting off the right edge with a lens of bare paper in its throat, a heavy gold diagonal slashing through it, and a green fan of curves meeting where that diagonal crosses a thinner blue one. Above it sits a clear equation: circle = slash + slash.

## Acceptance checks (encoding §11)

1. **ONLY STRAIGHT STRINGS, NO OUTLINE: PASS (png).** There is no curved blue or gold anywhere, and no rim, contour or shading. The silhouette exists only as string ends, and the top envelope reads cleanly. There is no solid single-colour band: blue strings beside the blue X at the right lip keep about 1 mm. The gcode collinearity check is left to the science critic.
2. **ONE PINCH POINT: PASS.** All the green members converge on the blue×gold crossing at about (178, 118) mm. Their ends near p are staggered on both sides (from the left at about 5, 9, 17, 22 and 30 mm, from the right at about 8, 12 and 18 mm), and one member passes straight through. There is no green blot.
3. **CIRCLE TO X, IN ORDER: FAIL.** Reading outward from p, no closed circle or closed ellipse can be found on paper. The loops break at both eye tips, at about (140, 168) and (258, 168), and elsewhere where they pass behind the lip. The bottom rim is cropped by the frame at y = 15, so the 31.72° ellipse's graze cannot be seen. The open members do leave through the frame crop honestly.
4. **THE EYE: PASS**, with a blemish. The lens is about 120 × 50 mm (≥ 45 mm tall), bounded by envelopes, and its interior holds zero ink. The lip fragments sit on the boundary (see dimension 4). **(A) The gold X-string is the longest straight line and is uninterrupted: FAIL.** At about 269 mm it is the longest line on the sheet, but it is **broken for about 14 mm** at the eye's lower-left lip, between about (145, 158) and (156, 146). The blue X is also broken, for about 12 mm at the lower-right lip, at about (236–248, 150–157).
5. **HONEST WORDS: PASS.** The text contains RATIONAL, PROJECTIVE, THEOREM (LEFSCHETZ 1924) (across a line break) and REAL DIMENSION 8. No colour is attached to H^{p,q}. The rebus reads green ○ = blue ╱ + gold ╲. The slants are +28° and −38°, against the X's +27° and −38°, so they are parallel. Deviation noted, not scored: the rebus is 22 mm tall and set top-right, where the encoding asked for 30 mm flush-left.

**AUTHORING §6 (reference as interpretation brief):**
1. Forms recognisable without fills: YES.
2. Lines follow the surface: YES, because the rulings are the surface.
3. Fine lines that are really two sides of one stroke: NO. On paper the gold X is one band. The blue X preview shows three hairlines, but at a 0.15 offset with a 0.2 nib they merge.

The interpretation beats r01's faithful plate: there is no inherited poster architecture, just one construction swallowing the frame, which is right for the Gabo branch.

## Biggest weakness

The punchline is cut. Both X strings are severed at the eye's lips (gold for about 14 mm, blue for about 12 mm), and the eye's two corners are knots of stubs. The heaviest, most important lines on the sheet therefore break exactly where the eye pulls focus. The pencil has no visibly closed loop either, so "a circle becomes the X" is carried by the rebus, not the construction.

## Mandates

1. **An unbroken X.** The gold index-0 string must run as one continuous band from its upper-left rim end to the frame crop. The blue must do the same from its rim end to the frame. They are currently broken at about (145–156, 146–158) and (236–248, 150–157). Fix this through the view (α0 / elevation / roll), never by drawing hidden stretches. Hold what v25 won: the green pinch at the crossing, blue visible length ≥ 0.8 × gold, and the crossing angle pushed from 65° into 70–110°. Test: trace each X string on the png and find no gap, and measure the angle between them.
2. **One closed loop and one visible graze.** The waist circle (ψ = 0) must show ≥ 85% of its length on paper with no gap > 8 mm. It is currently three arcs split at the eye tips (about (140, 168) and (258, 168)). If the bottom crop stays, the 31.72° ellipse must still be visibly closed, or its grazing rim must be inside the frame. Test: on the png, find a green curve through p that returns to p.
3. **Clean eye lips.** No blue or gold fragment may be shorter than 8 mm anywhere on the sheet. Resolve the two lip knots (left: x 125–155, y 140–175; right: x 235–282, y 145–175) by LOD at the envelope, not by occlusion stubs. Remove the lone blue dash on the upper lip at about (190, 188). Test: zoom each lip, count the stubs under 8 mm, and require 0.
