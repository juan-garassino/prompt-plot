## Biggest weakness
The plate's argument, "solid = only a sum", is proven in one of three lenses. The red disc shows it beautifully. The blue triangle never reaches a solid. The orange card's above-threshold region runs off its edges, so half its confetti looks unrendered. Three kernels read as one demonstration plus two decorations, and the Memphis ground that should make the scatter feel like a violation exists only in one corner.
