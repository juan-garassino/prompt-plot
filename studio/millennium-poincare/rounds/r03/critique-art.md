## Biggest weakness
The plate's edges and hinge are ragged in a plate whose whole argument is clean closure. The past family frays open at A's west pole and at B's crown and NE flank: tips stop at irregular distances and two float in the air. At the waist, orphan halo stubs and hooked ring tips crowd the red circle. The keyline is now right, but the eye is drawn to the loose ends around it, not to the landing that the occlusion rule is supposed to show.

## Mandates
1. **Make every past line visibly land.** At A's west pole (x 25–45, y 115–150), B's crown (x 170–200, y 278–298) and B's NE flank (x 225–245, y 225–275), each clipped halo line must end tucked against the line that covers it. That covering line is either the next-later halo line or the keyline's 3.05 mm moat edge. The ends should sit at a uniform clearance, as a consistent stepped tuck. Test on the gcode: every open pen-0 halo endpoint lies within 1.0 mm of another drawn black line, or within 3.5 mm of the keyline. It is now 1.8–2.0 mm, and 6.0 / 7.2 mm at (30.7, 124.3) and (235.0, 269.4). In the pole crops, the tips read as cards sliding under one another, with no loose hairs.
2. **Clear the waist to three elements: keyline, red circle, halo bundles.** The two 21–22 mm pen-0 halo stubs ending at (152.1, 199.7) and (177.9, 200.3) must either continue as full lines through the waist outside the moat, as r02's halo did, or be absent. The first A and B ring tips must not hook back toward the circle. Test: no pen-0 stroke shorter than 30 mm has any point within 15 mm of the cut centre (≈ 165, 200). In a 60 × 70 mm waist crop, each flank shows only continuous parallel halo lines, the keyline and the circle.
3. **Make the red points carry at 3 m, as §2 promises.** The extinction points go to Ø 2.6–3.0 mm solid (now ≈ 1.8 mm). They keep ≥ 5 mm of bare paper to the innermost ring where the geometry allows, and HANDOFF states the achieved clearance where it does not. This takes the deferred A5. Test: in the full-page phys preview scaled to 1414 px tall, both dots are ≥ 5 px across, and the red dot-to-ring bare gap is reported in HANDOFF for both lobes.
