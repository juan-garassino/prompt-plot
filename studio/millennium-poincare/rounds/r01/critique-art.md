## Biggest weakness
**The grey half-shell turns the abstract order into an object.** It is a 45 mm deep bowl with a dashed polar grid under A. It makes the A nest read as a lid on a pot, it causes all of the L0 floor violations (a pole asterisk plus bunched rim hairlines), and it outweighs B in area. The encoding asked for a crescent.

## Mandates
1. **Cut the shell down to a crescent or remove it.** The grey band must be ≤ 15 mm deep, measured perpendicular from A's lower-left nest edge (today it is ≈ 45 mm, reaching down to y ≈ 48).
