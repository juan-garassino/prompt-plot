## On the sheet
A dumbbell of black closed curves rises from lower left to upper right: a dense nest of rings in the large lobe, a lighter nest in the small one. At the narrow waist a small red ellipse marks the cut, crossed by a wider open red arc. A fine grey bowl sits below. Title and notes occupy the corners.

## The science
Each black curve is the whole space at one instant of a computed Ricci flow of a symmetric 3-sphere, drawn in section, so every chord stands for a round 2-sphere. The neck thins, is cut, and both pieces vanish, so the original was a sphere. The sphere reading, the 2D control and the word SOLVED are stated in text only.

## The science it encodes
Poincaré asked whether a closed 3-dimensional space in which every loop shrinks to a point must be the 3-sphere. Hamilton proposed deforming its geometry by Ricci flow, ∂g/∂t = −2 Ric. Perelman made it work by cutting the space where it develops a thin neck, capping the two ends, and continuing.

Every black curve is computed: Ricci flow of a rotationally symmetric, dumbbell-shaped 3-sphere. Each curve is the whole space at one instant, drawn as its meridian section, so each chord across it stands for a round 2-sphere. The lines are spaced at equal steps of Ricci time (Δt = 0.01) counted from the cut. The fan outside is the past: the neck races thinner (0.314 → 0.120, −62 %) while the big lobe loses only 12 %. The outline enclosing both nests is the surgery instant. The small crimson ellipse is the cut, two round caps of radius 0.12. Inside, each piece shrinks and turns round, and its gaps widen toward the end. The small piece vanishes at its red point at t = 0.117 after a few rings, the big one at t = 0.387 after many. Both pieces were spheres, so the original was one.

The wide red arc is the loop that curve-shortening cannot contract on the starting shape: it parks on the neck. The geometry shows the pinch and the order of the two extinctions. The 2-sphere reading, the 2-D control in which the neck would widen instead, and `SOLVED` are stated only in text.

## How it got here
This is round r01, the faithful thesis, built in seven trials. v4 tried a 35° tilt and was rejected, v6 thinned the crowded shell, and v7 clipped the rings to the keyline and enlarged `SOLVED`. Both critics failed it. Art said the grey half-shell turns the order into a "gourd on a bowl" and that the red arc ends mid-halo. Science objected that the footer's `RED RING … FOREVER` names the wrong red object and that the shell is uncaptioned. The parallel abstract round r02 ranked higher (flat section, no shell, no loop). Its iterate r03 is now the ledger's parent, and the shell and loop mandates (A6, S2, S3) are parked for a revived faithful flavour.
