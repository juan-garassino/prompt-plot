## Reads at a glance
Two black bullseyes in a lens, fed by red and blue dotted wings off striped wave ledgers. Below them, a busy routing diagram of green and violet packets. At 3 m it reads as an owl-faced transformer diagram.

## Acceptance checks
No `encoding.md` or `BRIEF.md` exists, so there is no §11. These are the AUTHORING §6 checks, with a reference in play:
1. Main forms recognisable without colour: **PASS**. The rings, packets and dome read in monochrome.
2. Shadow lines follow the surface: **PASS (n/a)**. There is no tonal shading, and the ring families follow the construction.
3. Fine lines that are really two sides of one thick stroke: **PASS**. None found.
4. Blackest regions intended: **FAIL**. The hero lens is intended. The ochre V knot, the overprinted ∂L/∂K pair (x 35–55, y 30–42) and the ochre dotted rope are accidental congestion.
5. Labels readable at pen width: **FAIL**. `nonlinearity` and `nonlinearity′` read "nonlhearty": the l-i-n cluster fuses. `softmax`, the fraction, the title and `∂L/∂A` all pass.
6. Thicker pen makes black knots: **FAIL**. The four FFN pinch nodes (≈ x 144/158/165/178, y 64 and y 35) ink solid wedges over their last ~2 mm. The Q/K fan tips also clump where trains cross (x 80–90, y 195–215).
7. Long empty travels or tiny marks with no benefit: **FAIL**. Travel is 67 % of draw across 4,751 pen cycles, and the scatter sprinkles random dots of mixed size through the rings. r03 proved 28 % travel.

## Biggest weakness
The concept stays a schematic, and J2 freezes that. Inside the frozen composition, converting to dots raised every leader to full weight while leaving the lower-third collisions in place: the V knot, the stacked ∂L pairs, the pinch knots and the bracket on the dome. As a result, the plate's hierarchy is flatter than v9's.

## Mandates
1. **Space or merge every same-pen dot bundle.** This covers the 5-train ochre rope from (105, 85) down to the Z packet at (97, 65), the crimson fan tips at x 78–92, y 190–215 and the blue fan tips at x 118–132, y 190–215. Converging trains either sit ≥ 2.0 mm apart centre-to-centre, or all but one end on a dot where they meet and one continues. Test: in a 4× crop of each zone, no two dot trains run < 2 mm apart for more than 5 mm, and no dots from different trains touch.
2. **Make `nonlinearity` and `nonlinearity′` legible at nib width.** Restore v9's letter spacing, or wider, so `l`, `i` and `n` are separate strokes with ≥ 0.8 mm clear between glyphs. Test: in the 4× crop of the labels at y≈55 and y≈26, the word reads "nonlinearity" and not "nonlhearty".
3. **Kill the FFN pinch-node knots, and clear the bracket from the dome.** Near each of the four pinch nodes, end every non-axis streamline where it comes within 0.8 mm of its neighbour, so only the axis line reaches the node. Drop the bracket legs so they end on the expand/project pinch nodes and never on a crest. Open ≥ 1.5 mm between the dome's dotted top envelope (x≈158, y≈73) and the bracket bar or `FFN`. Test: no solid violet wedge at any pinch in a 4× crop, and the black bracket touches no violet ink.

## Regressions vs compare-to (v9)
- **Type (DESCRIPTION § Keep "v9's type": NOT held).** In v9, `nonlinearity` and `softmax` were loosely tracked and legible. r04 sets them condensed, and `nonlinearity` and `nonlinearity′` read "nonlhearty". This is v5's fault coming back.
- **Hierarchy.** v9's leaders were whisper-light, so the packets and hero carried the sheet. r04's 1 mm dot trains are as heavy as the solid packet lines. The wings, the droplines under the hero, the softmax dotted tents and the ochre rope now compete with the figures they connect. J1 wanted this mark. The cost is that dot weight now needs spacing discipline (M1) to stay subordinate.
- **Halos (§ Keep "top half … hero intact": PARTIAL).** v9 (and the reference) closed the halos as ellipses. r04's A4(a) cut leaves four open ")( " parentheses that end in free air above and below the rings, so the field no longer reads as bounded wavefronts.
- **Scatter.** In v9 the scatter was fine grey specks. In r04 it is mixed-size filled dots, the largest ≈1.5 mm, and they land on the ring crests. The noise now reads as spatter on the hero.
- Still true from § Keep: the forward dome vs backward twin-peak fan (apart from the pinch knots), the ∂L/∂A 0.39 rhyme, and the single Z→FFN→Y axis.
