## 1. STYLE assignment

**BAUHAUS, Kandinsky's branch (Bauhausbuch 9, *Punkt und Linie zu Fläche*, 1926), in the
Millennium series palette: cream sheet, black plus one loud red, and a grey ghost.** The canon is
chosen for its ORDER. Pure SU(3) Yang–Mills has a spectrum in the (p, E) plane with exactly three
components, and each is literally one of Kandinsky's three elements. The vacuum is a **point**
(dimension 0). Each glueball is a **line** (a hyperbola, dimension 1). The two-glueball continuum
is a **plane** (dimension 2). The Bauhaus canon's "geometry as ideology, one scarce accent,
spaced caps" also *is* the series grammar. This is **not** the KANDINSKY SET colour-block look:
there are no primary planes and no checkerboards. We take the treatise's order, not its paint.
The plate is **flat by declaration**, for two reasons. The canon is flat, and the subject is a
plane: the joint spectrum of (H, P) is a set in the (p, E) plane. The Minkowski light cone
that the twist hijacks is already a 2D section. The 3D cup version (nested hyperboloids) is
rejected. Opaque cups hide the inner shells. Transparent cups overprint as coaxial ellipses.

## 2. The one-glance statement

> **The light cone's tip is empty. Nothing lies between the vacuum and the first mass.**

At 3 m the viewer sees a dark plane high on the sheet, fine curves below it that lean into a
faint diagonal, and then a large silence shut by one red curve, with a single point underneath.
At 30 cm the viewer finds that the silence is exactly as tall as the band of curves.

## 3. The abstract ORDER

**NESTED, read as POINT / LINE / PLANE.** A family of curves shares one pair of asymptotes (the
light cone), with an isolated point at the cone's apex and one continuous plane above them.

Exact mapping in one line each:
- "**Height is energy, width is momentum, and one scale serves both, so the cone is 45°.**"
- "**Point = the vacuum, lines = one-glueball mass shells E = √(p² + M²), plane = everything
  above E = √(p² + 4Δ²). Blank paper = no state.**"

## 5. Composition sketch — A3 portrait, 297 × 420 mm, margins 15, y measured UP from the bottom edge

Both theses share the same vertical order, which is also the reference's order and Kandinsky's.
From bottom to top: point, silence, lines, plane. Both use the same **1 : 1 : plane** proportion
from physics: the gap is s tall, the isolated band is s tall, and the plane lies above. A title
band sits at y ∈ [380, 405] for both. The plane is cropped at a straight implied line (y = 370,
strata ends, no drawn frame), so that **text never sits on or halos into the continuum**.

| 2 | PLANE | black 0.2 | the two-glueball continuum |

| 4 | SPECTRUM | black 0.3 | the discrete spectrum: vacuum point, 5 shells, threshold rim |

The order runs from light to dark with red last, so nothing inks over the accent. That makes

## 7. Expressive levers (one decision each)

- **Proportion.** Gap : isolated band = **1 : 1 exactly** (physics). Plane : lens area ≈ 1.7 : 1
  (faithful) and 1.8 : 1 (abstract), and the plane is the only tonal mass. No layout choice
  may disturb the 1 : 1.
- **Fill vs void.** There are three zones by physics: silence, lines, plane. There are also two
  kinds of blank: the enclosed lens (the gap) and the open spacelike field (relativity).
- **Density gradient.** No cosmetic ramp is allowed. The only gradient is kinematic: the shells
  converge on the cone as E − |p| ≈ M²/2p. The plane is uniform.
- **Colour play.** One scarce, loud red that *closes* the silence. The ghost grey is the sheet's
  only other hue, and it is quieter than black.
- **Texture direction.** The strata are horizontal (iso-energy) and run cross-grain to the
  rising diagonal. They never follow the hyperbolae, because shell-parallel texture would read as
  more particles (lie 6).

## 8. The twist

**What the viewer holds:** Minkowski's light cone, the icon of relativity, "where light lives".

**What the mechanism breaks:** in a theory whose classical waves *are* light-like, the cone is
**empty**. Nothing sits on it, and its tip holds only the vacuum. Every real state floats a finite
Δ above the tip. The idea is "massless equations, massive world" (CMI's own sentence). The plate
also quietly corrects the misconception that "the gap is the gluon's mass": no line on the
sheet is a gluon.

## 9. Forbidden list (binding on both theses; the science critic fails any of these)

1. **Any ink in the lens** other than the red Δ measure (and, in the faithful thesis only, the
   red Δ glyph on the axis). No hatch, no dashes, no texture, no gridline, no label, no halo.
2. **Anything on the cone as a state.** No solid cone, no line along E = |p|, no "gluon" label,
   no massless mode.
3. **Log or non-uniform E**, or different scales on E and p. The cone must measure 45°.
4. **Equal level spacing, a ladder, or a harmonic series.** The vertices are 1, 1.437, 1.550,
   1.720, 1.781, 1.856, 2.
5. **The vacuum as a ring, band, sea, wavy ground, bump or stem.** It is one solid Ø 3 mm point.
6. **Knots, tori, flux loops, instantons, lobed "specimens"**, or any glueball given a shape.
   No gold orbits, no dotted construction circles, no dash-dot centre lines.
7. **The continuum starting anywhere but exactly on E = √(p²+4)**. No strata between shells.
   No shell-parallel plane texture. No embedded shells above 2Δ.
8. **Axes, ticks, tick numbers, gridlines, arrows, a frame box, or a legend box.** The cone's
   rulings are the only frame, and Δ is the only measure.
9. **MeV or GeV anywhere**, "proven" or "theorem", mixing in the Chen data, or naming f₀(1710).
10. **Mirror symmetry about a centred axis** (faithful) or a drawn spine (abstract).
11. **Text on or haloed into the plane.** A halo would punch a false gap into the continuum.
12. **A second loud colour.** Red is the only accent, and the ghost grey stays quieter than black.
13. **3D cups or perspective.** Flatness is declared (§1).

## 10. Fabrication (Leo: F600 draw ≈ 10 mm/s, ~2.5 s per pen cycle with dwells)

- **Spacing floors.** The strata pitch is 1.0 mm ≥ 0.8 mm and ≥ 2.4 × 0.2 = 0.48 mm. The
  tightest shell pair is 3.76 mm (F) or 2.80 mm (A). The ghost dash gap is 4 mm. The strata inset
  from the rim is 0.29 mm. **Pitches and text sizes are physical.** On another paper, the geometry
  scales uniformly but the strata pitch stays 1.0 mm and the caps stay ≥ 1.8 mm. That means the
  strata count must be recomputed and the pitch never scaled.
- **Leo on A5 portrait** (`a5:portrait`, ×0.5): s = 50 mm/Δ. The tightest shells are then 1.88 mm (F)
  and 1.40 mm (A), so the floor still holds. The mandatory pen-up frame trace comes first.
- **Estimated time on A3:**

  | layer | faithful | abstract |
  |---|---|---|
  | GHOST | 378 mm dashed, ≈ 38 dashes → ≈ 2 min | 342 mm, ≈ 34 dashes → ≈ 2 min |
  | PLANE | 110 strata, ≈ 25.4 m → ≈ 42 min + 5 min of cycles | 120 strata, ≈ 19.0 m → ≈ 32 min + 5 min |
  | TEXT | ≈ 350 glyph strokes → ≈ 15 min (keep captions short) | same |
  | SPECTRUM | ≈ 1.72 m, 7 strokes + disc → ≈ 4 min | ≈ 1.66 m → ≈ 4 min |
  | RED | ≈ 0.65 m, ≤ 8 strokes → ≈ 2 min | ≈ 0.75 m → ≈ 2 min |
  | **total** | **≈ 70 min, 3 swaps** | **≈ 60 min, 3 swaps** |

- **Batching.** Strata are boustrophedon, bottom to top, with each stratum a single stroke
  ≤ 267 mm and a batch boundary every 20 strata (re-zero check). Shells go bottom to top, one
  stroke each, alternating direction. Cone dashes go apex-outward. Text is ordered by line. No
  sheet-crossing travel happens between consecutive strokes.
- **Flood risks.** The vacuum disc is the only intended solid. The rim/strata contact is handled
  by the inset. The red bar's 3 passes (abstract) run at 0.4 mm offset with a 0.5 nib, so they
  form a deliberate solid bar. Red must never land on the black disc: it starts at the disc edge.
- **Captions** (stroke font: uppercase, no Δ, √ or superscripts, so author those). Content is
  fixed and the wording may be tightened:
  - Title: `YANG-MILLS  MASS GAP`
  - Statement: `THE LIGHT CONE HOLDS ONLY ITS TIP, THE VACUUM. THEN NOTHING, UP TO Δ.`
  - Corner caption: `PURE SU(3) GAUGE THEORY. HEIGHT ENERGY, WIDTH MOMENTUM, ONE SCALE.
    MASSES: LATTICE, ATHENODOROU-TEPER 2020. 2++* SITS ON 2Δ; STATES ABOVE 2Δ OMITTED.`
  - Colophon: `CLAY PROBLEM: PROVE THE THEORY EXISTS ON R⁴ AND Δ > 0. OPEN.`
- **Lineage** (for HANDOFF/NOTES): `lineage: Wassily Kandinsky, Point and Line to Plane
  (Bauhausbuch 9, 1926). Order taken: point, line and plane as the three elements with weight
  and direction. Vacuum = point, one-glueball shells = lines, two-glueball continuum = plane.`
  The fallback (if the curator rejects the overlap with the KANDINSKY SET) is Riley's *Current*.
