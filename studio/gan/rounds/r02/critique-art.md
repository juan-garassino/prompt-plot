## Reads at a glance
A red-and-black stepped spiral winding out from a red plus inside a blue dotted circle, on crosshairs, with `NO FIXED POINT` bottom-left. At 3 m it reads as a phase plot with a title.

## Acceptance checks
(No encoding §11 exists. These are derived from HANDOFF and the rubric.)
- Lineage: "forces in alternation make the angular line, forces together make the curve". Alternating G/D legs read as angular stairs, and the f′≈0 crawl reads as a smooth arc. — **PASS**
- The outward spiral can be traced by eye end to end, hole to frame. — **PASS**
- The equilibrium is bare paper with one scarce accent `+`. — **PASS** (but the blue orbit now fences it)
- Accent pen scarce and loud. — **FAIL**: crimson is on every tread (half the drawn leg length). The scarce, loud pen is blue, and it is a legend category.
- No schematics: no axes, keys, or measured-caption furniture. — **FAIL**: full-page crosshair knives + symbol legend + parameter caption.
- Nothing crosses the margin. — **PASS**, though the last crimson tread ends exactly on the x=200 margin line. That reads as "stopped by the frame", not "left the arena".
- Label clearance ≥1.5 mm from geometry. — **PASS**, marginally: `18 STEPS` and `15 STEPS` sit in lanes barely wider than the type.
- Declared depth or declared flatness. — **FAIL**: neither.

## Biggest weakness
It is a figure, not a plate. The escape spiral is right, but it is presented on crosshair axes with a key and a parameter caption, centred like a target, and totally flat. The picture's own order (alternation → angle, agreement → curve, radius growing every turn) is explained by furniture instead of being built into the marks.

## Mandates
1. **Delete the figure furniture.** Remove the 4-row legend and its swatches entirely, and cut the `578 STEPS h 0.25 / r 0.74 TO 3.54` caption to at most one line set on the subtitle's y≈12 baseline. End both dotted sign-change knives at the outer ring's radius (no dotted line below y≈88, left of x≈20 or right of x≈195). Test: no legend glyph on the sheet, and no dotted mark outside the spiral's bounding circle.
2. **Make weight grow with radius so the spiral reads as advancing, not as a target.** Pass count on both G and D legs goes strictly 1 → 2 → 3 from the innermost turn to the outermost. The NE inner hairline stair and the 3-pass inner SW stair must no longer coexist on the same turn. Where an arm crosses a dotted knife, the knife breaks ≥1.5 mm each side so the arm lies OVER it. Test: on any radial line, treads get heavier going outward, and every arm/knife crossing shows a gap in the dots.
3. **Break the centred target.** Move the equilibrium `+` off the drawable centre to x≈80–85 (left of the title's right edge at x≈108) and keep y≈185–200. Scale so the last G tread runs off the right edge of the paper (cropped at the frame), not stopping on the margin. Close the empty y≈60–90 band by making the outer SW crawl arc pass within ≤8 mm of the title's cap-line. Test: the `+` is ≥20 mm left of the page's vertical centre; the final tread is clipped by the paper edge; the gap between the outer arc and `NO FIXED` is ≤8 mm.
