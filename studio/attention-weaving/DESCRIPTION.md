## In one line
Attention as **everything forced through one constriction** — faithful and mechanism are **interlaced** braids (Q and K weave above a softmax bead waist, V is spun into a green rope below; mechanism routes every strand from real GPT-2 L2 H9), while aperture ("SUMS TO ONE") transposes it to **potential flow through a single slit** — Q streamlines and K spirals are the elliptic coordinate system of one hole in a black wall, and softmax is the clump widths at the slit plus the profile standing on it.

## The science it encodes
- **aperture (r03 docstring + NOTES):** elliptic coordinates `x = ax + c·cosh φ·cos ψ`, `y = ay + c·sinh φ·sin ψ`; φ = 0 is the slit, so every streamline ψ = const crosses the wall inside the slit — "everything passes through one constriction" is a property of the coordinate system. Q_i = streamline ψ_i; K_j = a spiral that sweeps the whole Q fan then drops into the slit; s_ij = the crossing, over/under by sign(s_ij). Softmax is drawn twice: clump **width** p_j = round(a_j·N) filaments (Σp_j = N = 38) and profile **height** a_j over that clump. Z = AV: the cable gains gold where V is woven in; over/under at V × lane = sign(v_j[component]). Measured (`piece.LAST_STATS`): Σa = 1.0000000000, a_max = 0.190, H = 3.762 of 4.000 bits over 16 keys, partition tiles the slit 51.92 of 51.92 mm, 38 in / 38 out, min lane pitch 0.95 mm, 113 of 352 Q·Kᵀ crossings on the sheet, 366 V × lane crossings. The profile is a Fritsch–Carlson monotone interpolant with **two knots per clump at a_j** (flat tops) — so it is smooth only in the outer third of each clump; that is exactly why it still reads stepped. The attention weights are **solved to a target a_max (seeded), not GPT-2**. The "double sunburst" is only above the wall — below, the flow is a cable, not the φ < 0 mirror fan.
- **faithful (r01 NOTES):** a recreation of the reference with de-crowding; ~800 Q–K crossings resolved by score sign; strand count in = out; rope is three plies at a plottable pitch (≈ 45 mm wide vs the reference's ~30). Numbers seeded.
- **mechanism (r02 NOTES):** real GPT-2 small, **layer 2, head 9** (a coreference head) on "The pen plotter drew a black hole while the transformer watched itself think."; waist row q* = 12, token `itself`: transformer 0.4159, watched 0.3769, itself 0.1112, masked keys drawn as open rings; SUM = 1.000000000, top-3 = 0.904, effective support 3.96 of 13. 15 filaments per bundle (the real token count). Z = AV is the real product. On the sheet, V visibly emerges *from* the waist's left ribbon — contrary to the brief's "V joins after the waist, never through it".

## Weak — what doesn't

### aperture v19
- [craft] **The softmax profile still reads as a staircase**: flat plateaus with ~12 knot discs across the slit and one narrow flat-topped tower at u 0.40 — Juan's note 1 is not met; the two-knots-per-clump construction manufactures the steps it was meant to remove.
- [hierarchy] **The bottom half does not match the top** (Juan's note 2, still open): above, two families cross at wide angles and radiate; below, 16 parallel gold arcs meet a parallel green cable — two families of parallels, one crossing zone, then an 80 mm dead run-out to the right frame.
- [craft] The **tangle at the cable's bend** (0.58–0.67, 0.50–0.58): green lanes cross each other in short chevrons — reads as a plotting error.
- [concept] The "double sunburst" is single: the φ < 0 half of the coordinate system (the mirrored fan below the slit) is not drawn; only dotted ellipses hint at it. The twist ("the sunburst is a drain") would land harder if the drain's other side were the same order.
- [grid] Footer leading is inconsistent (line 1→2 ≈ 3 mm, then ≈ 8 mm); `SOFTMAX` label sits on the wall's arm away from the profile; the cumulative curve has no label since `CUMULATIVE 1.000` was dropped.
- [space] The lower-left void (u 0.04–0.30, v 0.55–0.72) is leftover between the gold and the title rather than shaped.

### faithful v15
- [space] The **rope's pretzel knot** in the lower right (0.44–0.90, 0.70–0.90) is a heavy tangle of loops — collision, not braid; it outweighs the Q·Kᵀ weave that is the subject.
- [concept] It is the reference with its drafting furniture (compass circles, dotted rules, open circles) — decoration that carries nothing; the softmax is a dotted panel, not a throat.
- [hierarchy] The waist (0.44–0.56, 0.43–0.53) is a rectangle, not the narrowest point; the constriction does not feel inevitable.

### mechanism v20
- [craft] Title's final `G` runs onto the right margin; the K hairpin loop at (0.15–0.27, 0.18–0.33) reads as a knot error.
- [concept] V visibly emerges from the waist's left ribbon, violating "V only after the waist" — the order of operations is wrong on the sheet.
- [space] Gold figure-8 over the left half (0.05–0.45, 0.52–0.72) and the gold/green braid lower right are both dense and equal — two competing masses, no quiet zone except the bottom band.
