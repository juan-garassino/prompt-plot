## Biggest weakness
The lower half is still the weaker half, and it now also eats the sheet's quiet zone. Gold is back to 16 near-horizontal parallels entering from the left, which was v19's "two families of parallels", now crossing a fan. Where the green bundle tightens (x 95–140, y 115–165), gold is shredded into sub-5 mm crumbs. About 12 gold arcs then stop dead in open paper at x 140–190, y 150–175. The top half radiates *into* one point with wide-angle weaving. The bottom has no equivalent gesture, and it has filled the bare left-middle band that made the r04 storm read louder.

## Mandates
1. **Gold becomes one sweeping, closed family, and the left-middle void comes back.**
   - Every gold strand runs from its left reed tick diagonally down-right and ends on a green lane or on the right/bottom reed. None may stop in open paper (currently x 140–190, y 150–175).
   - No gold piece shorter than 5 mm anywhere. Where green pitch is under ~2 mm (x 95–140), gold passes under the whole tight bundle in one gap.
   - The rectangle x 10–90, y 100–150 is bare paper. Test: crop that rectangle and count gold pieces under 5 mm; both counts must be zero.
2. **Make the throat the narrowest, loudest thing again.**
   - Cut the slit back to ≤ 52 mm, as in r04. The hill's base spans the full slit.
   - Delete the raised shelf and the jamb steps at x≈67 and x≈141, so the profile rises directly off the wall's cut edges.
   - Delete the `Q11 · ITS ROW IS THE HILL` caption from the storm; the fact goes in the footer.
   - Test: slit width ≤ 52 mm; no horizontal black run between the wall and the profile; no text in the upper field except `Q` and `K`.
3. **Giant type and reed mouths at plotter craft.**
   - Every stroke of `SUMS / TO ONE` gets the same construction and weight. The `M` and `N` diagonals match the stems. Multi-pass strokes are filled, not striped, at 30 cm: pass pitch ≤ 0.4 mm, or a serpentine fill inside the letter outline.
   - No two strands end on one reed tick; mouths are ≥ 3 mm apart (top edge x≈43/63/85/143/210, right edge y≈229).
   - The green strand under `Z = AV` routes clear of the label by ≥ 2 mm, or the label moves onto the `TO ONE` baseline in open paper.
   - Test: zoom each letter, each reed and the label.
