## In one line
The double slit drawn as an **interfering lattice** — the cross term 2Re(ψ₁ψ₂*) is drawn nowhere; it is the bare paper where probability was taken from and the extra density where it was put, with the count conserved. r01 extrudes the two intensity laws as stacked oblique reliefs (dune vs. comb); r02 strings the same 24 equal-probability beads on two abacus wires; r03 lays 44 equal-probability rules on an oblique slab so spacing is the only variable; r04 doubles that ruling in two pens at a 7° offset, warped by the coherence envelope, carrying the display sentence ONE AND ONE MAKE NONE.

## Lede
The double slit drawn so that **interference moves probability rather than creating it**: the bare gaps and the extra density balance exactly.

## On the sheet
Four related plates. One stacks two oblique reliefs, a smooth single bell above a five-fringed comb, in black with crimson plumb lines at the nulls. Another strings equal beads on two wires, with crimson beads marking missing places. A third lays equal-spaced rules across a slab. The last weaves crimson and green rulings into a moiré around a giant black word.

## The science
All four share one calculation of light through two slits, using the exact aperture integral, with slit separation three times the width so the third fringe order vanishes. The cross term itself is never drawn; it appears as paper left bare and as extra density elsewhere. In the moiré plate the fringe structure is not readable.

## The science it encodes
All four share one solver (cite `r01/NOTES.md` §1–2): λ = 633 nm, slit a = 100 µm, separation d = 300 µm = 3a, L = 2.000 m; one-slit amplitudes from the exact Rayleigh–Sommerfeld aperture integral, 96-node Gauss–Legendre, `r−L` formed as `(x−x')²/(r+L)` to keep phase digits. I_quantum = |ψ₁+ψ₂|², I_classical = |ψ₁|²+|ψ₂|², cross term Δ = 2Re(ψ₁ψ₂*) drawn nowhere. Receipts: max I_cl / max I_q = 0.500000000000; nulls at (m+½)a/d within 1.2×10⁻⁴; visibility 0.99992; ∫Δ / ∫I_cl over the crop = 2.25×10⁻⁴. d = 3a puts the third principal maximum on the first envelope zero — the missing order. Nothing is seeded (s3/s7/s21 byte-identical).
- r01 draws the two laws on one height scale, so the 2:1 comb-to-dune height is physics.
- r02: 24 beads per wire, bead k at the inverse CDF (k+½)/24 of each law; bead size set so the brightest fringe packs solid (docstring; the sheet shows a packed central run, not visibly "exactly full").
- r03: 44 rules at the equal-probability quantiles; channels = fringe zeros u=(m+½)/3, wide end zones = envelope zeros u=±1.
- r04: 42 rules, laid twice at β = 7°; one displacement field whose amplitude is the coherence envelope 2|ψ₁||ψ₂| warps both ruling and type (docstring). On the render, the fringe channels are NOT readable as bare lanes — the warp and the letter knockouts dominate — so the claimed "cancellation lattices left as bare paper" does not visibly land.

## How it got here
- **r01 (double_slit v1–v13, s3/s7/s21)**: v4 drew both reliefs as dense vertical-fin meshes with crimson fall-lines inside the dune and a small single-line title; v7 introduced the giant stacked `DOUBLE / SLIT` + standfirst and coarsened the meshes; v10 switched to the cabinet-oblique extrusion with a cross-grid mesh, dune as a thin slab; s7/v13 replaced the grid with rims + diagonal fall hatch, raised the comb and made crimson a depth-0 plumb/tick system; the red note changed from `RED  THE ZERO SET OF THE QUANTUM LAW` to the two-line plumb note. Self-score in NOTES: avg ≈ 7.1, tension 5 ("symmetric and centred"); recommended landscape, a crop, and the Englert D²+V²≤1 morph along depth.
- **r02 (ds_abacus v1–v5)**: v1 a flat parallelogram tray with 40 tiny beads and a crimson plumb forest (portrait); v2 → v4 cut the count 20 → 16 → 12 and grew the beads and a 3D box; v5 flipped to landscape, 24 beads, the box cropped at the right drawable edge. Gained scale and a readable bead gap; it is still an object (an abacus).
- **r03 (ds_lattice v1)**: dropped the object for the pure ruling on a slab — the rubric's "abstract order" answer; one render only.
- **r04 (ds_psy v1–v4)**: v1–v3 read `ONE / AND ONE / MAKE NONE` clearly in bold warped type over a strong red/green moiré band; v4 enlarged `NONE` and let the knockouts erase `ONE AND ONE MAKE` — the sentence (the joke) was lost.
- Juan's feedback: none recorded.

## Keep — what works

### double_slit (r01)
- The 2:1 height ratio between the comb's central peak (v≈0.51) and the dune's crest, on one scale — the "same stuff, rearranged" read lands without text.
- Crimson plumbs standing on the dune at the four null positions with nothing under them on the comb — the cross term as absence.
- `INTERFERENCE / REDISTRIBUTES / IT DOES NOT / CREATE` as the standfirst.

### ds_abacus (r02)
- The same 24 beads on both wires, crimson plumbs dropping into bare wire at u 0.48 and 0.61 — conservation readable at a glance.
- The empty field between the wires (v 0.33–0.57) crossed only by dashed crimson — a composed quiet zone.
- Landscape format and the frame cropping at the right edge — first variant with a real asymmetry.

### ds_lattice (r03)
- The rule: every rule identical, position is the only variable — the clearest statement of "probability rearranged, count conserved" in the family.
- Bands, channels and the two wide bare end zones on the front face (u 0.14–0.49) — the missing order as blank paper.
- Crimson only on the two outermost rules — scarce and loud.

### ds_psy (r04)
- The twist: `ONE AND ONE MAKE NONE` — a proverb the mechanism completes (two amplitudes summing to zero). Best line in the family.
- Two pens beating at a small angle so the moiré physically happens on paper — interference drawn, not depicted.
- The warp amplitude tied to the coherence envelope: calm at top/bottom, melted in the middle.

## Weak — what doesn't

### double_slit (r01)
- [concept] Two extruded intensity reliefs with labelled stages, a registration band and a three-column colophon — the scientific figure; a hostile read is an axis plot in 3D.
- [tension] Both reliefs are mirror-symmetric masses centred on the sheet; nothing crops the frame (the NOTES' own weakest dimension).
- [craft] Hidden-line debris on the comb's steep inner flanks (u 0.38–0.48, v 0.63–0.73): broken hatch fragments crossing each other; the dune's back rim goes dashed on one flank only.
- [hierarchy] Giant `DOUBLE / SLIT` competes with the comb for first read.
- [space] `THIRD ORDER MISSING` presses against the right drawable edge.

### ds_abacus (r02)
- [concept] It is an illustration: a nameable object (an abacus) carrying the mechanism — fails § 6's "if a viewer can name an object, cut it".
- [craft] The inner back post at u≈0.21 runs through `WHICH PATH` and `PSI1`; the frame's back rails graze the drawable edge at u 0.97 as a collision, not a decided crop.
- [hierarchy] The frame's empty rectangles are the largest ink; beads (the data) are small at 3 m.
- [grid] Middle caption column mixes two type sizes within one block.
- [depth] The box's oblique rails are the only depth; beads and wires are flat.

### ds_lattice (r03)
- [craft] On the top face the dense band compresses to near-solid black (perpendicular spacing well under the 0.8 mm floor); the top-right note at ~1.2 mm caps clogs into unreadable blobs.
- [hierarchy] The ruling occupies only u 0.14–0.49 of a slab that spans u 0.04–0.80 — the box outline, not the ruling, sets the mass; the ruling is too small to dominate.
- [space] The empty lower-right quadrant is leftover, not shaped — nothing pushes against it.
- [concept] The crimson dashed diagonals on the top face float in blank paper; "where the third order should have stood" is not legible as a position in the lattice.
- [depth] The slab is a thin oblique card; depth carries no variable.

### ds_psy (r04)
- [concept] The sentence is gone: `ONE AND` reads as "UIVE AIVU", `ONE MAKE` is erased, only `NONE` survives — the joke needs all five words.
- [concept] The fringe channels (the actual cancellation) are not readable as bare lanes; the ruling reads as a generic wave field.
- [craft] Letter halos chop the rules into short dash clusters (u 0.1–0.9, v 0.3–0.6) that look like hatching errors; 20.6 m draw, 17.5 m travel.
- [hierarchy] Type and ruling fight at equal weight in the middle; there is no clear second read.
- [grid] Title, sentence and footer share no axis.

## Next versions
1. **ONE AND ONE MAKE NONE** (abstract) — Take the r03 ruling (one pen, equal-probability rules, position is the only variable) and set the proverb as giant type whose counters are the bare channels: the words sit exactly on the fringe zeros so the cancellation lanes ARE the letter gaps, and the missing third order is the blank where a sixth word would go. Keep r04's two-pen 7° beat only inside the bright bands. Scores higher on concept (a twist the physics finishes), hierarchy (one sentence, one ruling) and craft (no halo chop).
2. **ERASER** (mechanism) — The r01 NOTES' strongest idea: one ruling (not a relief) whose depth axis is distinguishability D with V = √(1−D²); at the front edge the rules bunch into fringes, at the back they relax to the smooth envelope, so the whole plate is one object morphing from interference to which-path, cropped asymmetrically at the frame. Real information on every axis, a working diagonal, no figure furniture.
3. **THE COUNT** (lens) — Two rulings of the same 44 rules, classical above and quantum below, with every rule joined to its partner by one straight line: bands where the connectors converge, bare fans where they diverge, crimson for the rules pushed farthest. Interlaced/flow order that shows redistribution as motion, replacing the abacus object with the pure mapping.
