## Biggest weakness
The hard crop cut the hero's eye off the paper. The one mass meant to own the sheet is now a radial fan with no centre. The spiral, the plate's subject, has moved to rung 1 in the middle of the page, and there it is just one more round medallion on the string. The hierarchy that the whole ladder depends on (big → ½ → ¼ … → point) now has no visible first term.
