## Reads at a glance
A big pale blue whirlpool at top left shrinks down a straight diagonal into four ever-darker, smaller copies, ending at an empty red circle. A black target sits alone at top right.

## Acceptance checks (encoding §11)
1. **Rings vs spiral at 3 m: PASS.** There are 20 closed concentric strokes (22 were specified, 20 is within the Δψ tolerance) with no ends and no spiral. The hero is an inward spiral. Bare paper from the disc to the hero rim is about 47 mm and to rung 1 about 72 mm. The two never share a texture.
2. **The ladder is exact: PASS (at preview precision).** There are 5 rungs with R ≈ 80 / 40 / 20 / 10 / 5 (2:1 within about 1 mm read error). The centres (62,272) → (155,163) → (201,107) → (225,80) → (238,66) → L (248.3, 52.4) are collinear within read error. The core arms curl at a shallow angle into the circle in rungs 0–2. The stroke-for-stroke ×2 overlay is for the science critic to measure.
3. **The pen floor is honest: PASS.** Every rung has a bare eye. The hero's eye is about 1.5–2 mm, a little under the stated ≈2.9 mm. The nearest blue is 13.08 mm from the red centre against a ring radius of 11.5 mm, so there is no blue inside it. Red appears only in the ring and the stamp. The minimum blue–blue gap is ≥ 0.8 mm.
4. **Hierarchy and silence: PASS.** The hero has about 4× the area of the disc and of rung 1. The lower-left zone (x 15–140, y 30–165) is bare and is about 1/6 of the drawable area. No caption sits on lines.
5. **Status wording: PASS.** It reads "CLAIMED 8 SEP 2026: BLOW-UP WITH A SMOOTH PUSH / FORCED CASE NOT YET VERIFIED · CLAY: NO AWARD / WITHOUT A PUSH: OPEN". Nothing in the 2D disc says large → small ("THE EYE ONLY OPENS").

AUTHORING §6 (reference in play):
1. Forms recognisable without colour: **yes.**
2. Lines follow the form rather than a random mesh: **yes.** Every blue line is a streamline.
3. Fine lines that are really two sides of one thick stroke: **no.**
4. Blackest regions intended: **no, in the preview.** The darkest mass is the black disc, which is an artefact of the uniform-weight preview. The 0.1 mm hairline will not be black on paper.
5. Labels readable at the real pen: **yes.** "4/3 T0" is gone.
6. Knots from a thicker pen: **no.** The cores of rungs 3–4 stay open.
7. Long travels or pointless tiny marks: **travel is heavy** (13.5 m). Tiny marks are few (4 blue under 3 mm).

As an interpretation: the reference is one continuous gesture, a wave feeding a vortex. That has become a disc cut out at 4δ. The Riley surface lives inside the hero, but its rim is a circular comb, so every rung reads as a badge.

## Biggest weakness
Every vortex is cut out as a circle and set in white. The eye reads a string of blue badges rather than one surface that turns. Of the five, the hero is the lightest: it is cropped shallowly on one frame and is tonally paler than rungs 1–2. So the mass that should own the sheet reads as a pale cut-out, and the darkest blue lands mid-sheet on rung 1.
